Who We Are

Kenya's trusted agribusiness products & services company

Born from a conviction that Kenya's smallholder farmers deserve access to professional-grade agricultural technology, Sisili Agritech was established in Nairobi in 2021 to close the gap between world-class equipment and the farmers who need it most.

Today we are a professionally managed company in the agribusiness and supply chain industry — supplying, installing, commissioning and supporting agricultural equipment, irrigation systems, dairy machinery and agritech solutions for smallholder farmers, cooperatives, NGOs and county programmes across Kenya.

With a national footprint and a dedicated team of experts, we combine field-level experience with research-backed technical rigour to deliver solutions that produce measurable results on the farm.

01
Certified & Research-Backed
Our EBK-licensed engineers and NEMA-registered experts work alongside the University of Nairobi to deliver solutions grounded in verified science and real field data — not guesswork.
02
Fully Managed, Every Stage
We go beyond supplying equipment. Our team handles site assessment, technical design, installation, operator training, and ongoing after-sales maintenance — all under one engagement.
03
Built for Kenya's Farmers
Every solution we specify is calibrated for East Africa's diverse agro-ecological conditions. We understand the terrain, the crop cycles, and the unique challenges facing farmers in each region.

Our Clients

Who we serve across East Africa

We work with clients at every scale — from smallholder cooperatives in Laikipia and Meru to international development agencies and county governments across Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.

🌾

Smallholder Farmers

Individual farmers and cooperatives seeking reliable water access, reduced input costs, and on-farm mechanization — from semi-arid lowlands to highland tea zones.

How we help
Solar irrigation design, mechanization access, and hands-on field training to increase yields and cut energy costs.
🤝

NGOs & Development Agencies

International and local organizations implementing food security, climate adaptation, and rural livelihood programmes.

How we help
Technical design, Environmental & Social Impact Assessment (ESIA), Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) framework development, and community training to ensure projects achieve measurable impact.
🏛️

Government Bodies

National and county government agencies managing irrigation schemes, agricultural extension, and environmental regulation.

How we help
Policy-aligned engineering designs, National Environment Management Authority (NEMA)-compliant Environmental & Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) reports, and capacity-building for extension officers.
🔬

Research Institutions

Universities and research institutes requiring field-based engineering expertise and applied data from live agricultural systems across East Africa.

How we help
Research collaboration, field data collection, joint grant applications, and practical field exposure for students in applied agri-engineering.
