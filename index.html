Featured Project

AAKTP: Agri-Aqua Knowledge Transfer Partnership

A multi-institutional research project co-developed with Aston University (UK), Çukurova University (Turkey), and the University of Nairobi — the AAKTP project demonstrated that hybrid solar-diesel irrigation systems can dramatically reduce operating costs and carbon emissions for smallholder irrigation in semi-arid East Africa.

The research validated Sisili's core engineering model: that renewable energy integration in agricultural water systems is not just environmentally beneficial, but economically transformative for the smallholder farmer.
