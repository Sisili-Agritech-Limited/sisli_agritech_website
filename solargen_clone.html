Energy

Solar is by far a superior source of power. It is cheap and readily available in every African village. It is our dream to see underserved communities in Africa having access to clean, affordable, and sustainable energy.

Irrigation

From a small plot to a large commercial farm, we can help you irrigate cost effectively without fossil fuels. Our aim is to contribute to food security in Africa through climate adoptive technologies.

Water

The biggest challenge for pastoral communities is access to sustainable clean water and grazing land. Solargen aims to make access to clean water affordable and sustainable for these underserved communities.
